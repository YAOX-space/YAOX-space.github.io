Research Experience

Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Distribution Power Flow Estimation
Apr 2025 – Present
Advisor: Prof. Ruisheng Diao · ZJU-UIUC Institute, Zhejiang University
High penetration of distributed energy resources increases system nonlinearity in distribution networks; traditional power flow algorithms suffer from low efficiency and poor physical consistency in large-scale networks.
  • Built a PINN surrogate model integrating power flow equations and physical constraints for fast distribution network power flow estimation.
  • Conducted simulation tests on IEEE 123 and IEEE 8500 benchmark systems, validating accuracy and computational efficiency advantages.
  • Extending the model to transient simulation and large-scale complex systems; preparing a follow-up journal submission.
Formal Verification of Neural Networks via auto_LiRPA
Oct 2025 – Present
Advisor: Prof. Huan Zhang · ECE Department, UIUC
Robustness verification of deep neural networks in safety-critical systems remains computationally expensive with insufficient bound tightness in existing methods.
  • Studying α/β-CROWN optimization strategies, conducting experimental analysis on bound tightness and computational efficiency.
ML-Based Electricity–Carbon Coupling and Emission Prediction
Apr 2025 – Jun 2025
Advisor: Prof. Ruisheng Diao · ZJU-UIUC Institute, Zhejiang University
Modeling the nonlinear coupling between power load and carbon emissions under high renewable energy penetration.
  • Led the overall project design; developed multivariate time-series models using LSTM/DNN, incorporating meteorological data, historical load, and electricity behavior features.
